    Konform WCAG audit platform

    Desktop web · Enterprise SaaS · AI triage

    Accessibility scanners report symptoms page by page. Konform reports causes - and turns a 3,187-row backlog into 142 decisions. One row is one fix: every contrast failure traced to the same ghost button is a single change, not ninety-four. What used to be a sprint of spreadsheet work is now an afternoon of triage.

My profile. What I bring:

  • Legal precision

    My law degree taught me to read the fine print and spot the edge case nobody covered. I turn dense requirements into clear design rules.

  • Business sense

    Freelancing for EU and US clients taught me to think in budgets, deadlines and results. I start with the outcome, not the screen.

  • End-to-end ownership

    I take work from first brief to launch, and I’m still there afterwards for the fixes and updates nobody planned for.

  • Honest design

    No dark patterns or manipulative consent banners. Honest flows build lasting trust, and under EU law they’re also the safer choice.

  • Discretion

    My client and in-house work is under NDA, and it stays that way. Unreleased products, roadmaps and internal figures are safe with me.

  • Collaboration and handoff

    Aligning engineering, product and operations, then handing off token-based specs and documented states developers can build from.
